How To Fit A Watch Strap

Posted on February 06, 2014 by Victor & Wells

What might seem at first a tricky job, is relatively simple once you get to grips with the process.

1. Make sure you have the correct size strap. We have written a blog post on that here.

2. You will need a spring bar tool in order to remove the spring bars on your watch, available here.

3. Remove the existing bracelet on your watch strap. This involves using your spring bar tool to push down on the springs in the spring bar. You can find them by looking at the back of your watch where the strap connects to the watch. In the small holes you should be able to see a bar with two ridges on it. Place your spring bar between these two ridges and then push parallel to the spring bar to release the spring bar from the watch.

4. Once the strap is removed, if you are installing a NATO or Zulu strap you will need to place the spring bars back into the watch without a strap. If you are installing a watch strap such as a rubber, leather or new metal strap then you will need to place the spring bars into the strap and then fit them into the watch to secure the strap. 

5. Once these steps are complete your watch strap should be installed! Double check the spring bars are fully secure and enjoy.

Please note we cannot accept any damages due to following these instructions.
